This page outlines the commercial framework for Enterprise and business customers who wish to license Identical or Custom architectural models, designs, control systems, or related deliverables.

---

## 1. How Ordering Works

1. Review the public (or private) catalog of models and designs.
2. Open a GitHub issue with the label `enterprise-inquiry` **or** contact the Licensor directly with:
   - Desired model / design numbers
   - Identical Copy or Custom Variant request
   - Approximate deployment scale and use case
3. Licensor responds with a draft Statement of Work (SOW) and commercial proposal.
4. Upon agreement, a formal Contract + SOW is executed.
5. Invoices are issued according to the payment schedule in the SOW.
6. Deliverables are released after the agreed initial payment (or as otherwise stated in the SOW).

## 3. Payment Terms

- **Currency:** United States Dollars (USD) unless otherwise agreed.
- **Invoicing:** Issued upon SOW signature or at defined milestones.
- **Due Date:** Net 15 or Net 30 from invoice date (stated in SOW).
- **Late Payment:** Interest may apply at the rate stated in the Contract / SOW.
- **Taxes:** Licensee is responsible for any applicable sales, use, VAT, or similar taxes.

## 4. Accepted Payment Methods

Currently supported or planned methods (confirm at time of engagement):

## 5. Refunds & Cancellation

- Support subscriptions may be cancelled at the end of the then-current term.

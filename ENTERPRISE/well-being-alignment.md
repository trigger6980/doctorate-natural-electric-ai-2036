# Well-Being Alignment (Enterprise Language)

This page states how commercial language in this portfolio is supposed to stay attached to the Natural Electric + Future AI mission. It is not a marketing scorecard and it does not claim outcomes.

## Mission the contract is allowed to repeat

- Build inspectable, energy-honest operators for places the grid does not reliably reach.
- Keep public research public; sell only what a written contract names.
- Treat human safety (combustion, potable water, electrical isolation) as outranking feature velocity.
- Refuse work — in software and in sales copy — when joules, measurements, or licenses are missing.

## Claims a cover letter may make

- That the public catalog and host tests exist and can be run.
- That identical vs custom is a real scope split.
- That energy budgets in the cards are interfaces or placeholders until measured.
- That Operator AI can *skip* tasks when a broker refuses a grant.

## Claims a cover letter may not make

- Customer logos, revenue, headcount, or “deployed at scale.”
- Certified safety listings for generators or appliances.
- Measured kWh, COP, gallons-per-hour, or crop yield from this repository.
- That a model card is a trained-weight package or a mesh energy market.
- SLA / uptime numbers (none have been operated as a service metric).

## Well-being tests before a quote is drafted

1. Does the request ask us to hide an energy cost or over-claim a generator?
2. Does the request need combustion or potable-water work that this repo only sketches?
3. Can the buyer audit the delivered operator (task graph, checkpoints, refusal paths)?
4. Is any biome / food / water coupling being sold as a self-sufficiency guarantee?

If (1) or (4) is yes, the honest next step is to narrow scope or decline — not to invent a case study.

No prices, customers, or SLAs are added by this page.

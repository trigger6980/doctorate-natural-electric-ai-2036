# Enterprise & Business

This folder contains the commercial documents for organizations that wish to license Identical or Custom versions of the architectural models, control systems, off-grid designs, and related work.

## Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) | Full draft Enterprise & Business Contract template for review and negotiation |
| [PAYMENT-BILLING.md](PAYMENT-BILLING.md) | Payment terms, indicative pricing framework, invoicing process, and billing contact information |
| [order-and-contract.md](order-and-contract.md) | Original high-level order process (still valid as a quick overview) |

## How to Proceed

1. Read the Contract and Payment & Billing pages.
2. Open a GitHub issue with the label `enterprise-inquiry` (or contact directly) describing what you need.
3. A Statement of Work and commercial proposal will be prepared for discussion.
4. Upon agreement, the formal Contract + SOW are executed and invoicing begins.

Repositories may remain private until payment and billing infrastructure is fully operational.

---

Questions about enterprise licensing or billing can be raised via GitHub issues or the contact method provided in any active SOW.

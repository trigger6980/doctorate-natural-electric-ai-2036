# Literature & Project Map — Natural Electric × Future AI (2036 Horizon)

Living document. Update with every major paper, open-source project, or hardware demonstration that advances ambient energy + intelligent edge systems.

## Energy Harvesting Foundations
- MIT magnetic-field energy harvesting self-powered sensors (2024).
- Indoor photovoltaics (DSSC, OPV, perovskite) under lux-level lighting + EdgeML demonstrations.
- Hybrid PV + ambient RF + supercapacitor + RL-scheduled TinyML for energy-neutral IoT (2026-era results).
- Vibration / triboelectric / multi-contact TVEH high-power-density harvesters.

## Off-Grid & Portable AI
- Solar-powered Raspberry Pi Zero LLM nodes.
- Hand-crank + solar AI-in-a-box designs (portable, offline LLM capability).
- Microgrid-aware edge clusters (higher power, still renewable-first).

## TinyML & Energy-Aware Inference
- Quantized models on ESP32 / Cortex-M class devices with known mJ costs.
- On-device LSTM / RF / tiny-NN for irradiance forecasting and duty-cycle control.
- Reinforcement learning policies that keep nodes energy-neutral under stochastic harvest.

## Agentic & Physical AI Directions (toward 2036)
- Multi-agent orchestration under partial observability and energy constraints.
- Neuromorphic and physical-AI co-design where energy and information are harvested together.
- Formal or probabilistic guarantees of perpetual or near-perpetual operation.

- llama.cpp / MLC-LLM / executorch-style on-device runtimes.
- ESP32 / Zephyr / MicroPython energy-aware frameworks.
- Open hardware solar + MPPT + supercap reference designs.

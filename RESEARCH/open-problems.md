# Open Problems (Natural Electric × Future AI)

1. **Simultaneous energy + information harvesting** — Can the same physical transducer that extracts joules also extract high-value sensory information with minimal extra cost?
2. **Formal energy contracts for agents** — How do we give multi-agent systems verifiable guarantees that they will not deplete shared or individual energy stores under adversarial conditions?
3. **Neuromorphic Natural Electric** — Mapping ambient energy signals directly into spiking or event-driven compute so that power and computation are co-located.
4. **Scalable offline knowledge** — Maintaining useful, updatable knowledge bases on highly constrained, intermittently powered nodes without cloud round-trips.
5. **Human-scale reproducibility** — Designs that a skilled independent researcher or home-lab builder can reproduce with commodity parts and open tools.
6. **Security under energy starvation** — Maintaining integrity, authentication, and safe failure modes when nodes must sleep for long periods or operate with extremely limited joules.

These problems guide the long-term research roadmap of this repository.

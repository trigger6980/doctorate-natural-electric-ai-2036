# Generator 02 — Rocket-Mass / Biomass Hybrid — Safety-Critical Design

**Dyad process:** Lumen explored high-efficiency heat capture and thermal-mass integration. Aegis enforced combustion safety, carbon-monoxide risk, clearances, and operator training as non-negotiable.

## 1. Core Safety Principles (Non-Negotiable)
- Complete combustion before heat extraction (rocket design exists to achieve this).
- Negative pressure / proper draft at all times during operation.
- No flammable materials within required clearances.
- Carbon-monoxide monitoring in occupied spaces.
- Pressure and temperature relief on any closed water loop.
- Operator must understand fuel, draft, and shutdown procedures.

## 2. Critical Dimensional & Design Guidelines
- Combustion chamber and riser sized so that gases reach high temperature and complete combustion before entering the heat-exchange section.
- Heat exchanger (water jacket or copper coil) placed after the hot riser, never choking the exhaust path.
- Flue / exhaust must terminate outdoors with proper height and clearances from windows, intakes, and combustibles.
- Thermal mass (if used) should be designed so surface temperatures remain safe to touch after the burn or are clearly guarded.
- Minimum clearances to combustibles: follow local code; typically 45–90 cm or more depending on insulation and design — verify with tested rocket-mass heater guidelines.

## 3. Water-Side Safety
- Closed-loop systems require ASME-rated or equivalent pressure-relief and temperature-relief valves.
- Expansion tank sized for the water volume and maximum temperature.
- Never allow the heat exchanger to become the sole pressure vessel without relief.
- Prefer a heat-exchanger coil inside a vented or properly protected storage tank.

## 4. Fuel & Operation Rules
- Use only dry, appropriately sized biomass (small-diameter wood or tested agricultural waste).
- Never leave an operating system completely unattended until draft and temperatures are stable and understood.
- Install a CO alarm in any adjacent living space.
- Provide a clear shutdown and emergency procedure (close air intake, ensure draft continues until cool).

## 5. Hybrid Integration with Solar
- Solar thermal as primary; rocket-mass as backup or peak-load provider.
- Shared storage tank with two heat-exchanger coils or careful plumbing prioritization.
- Control logic (Operator AI / energy-aware layer) should prefer solar when available and only signal biomass need when tank temperature falls below threshold and solar forecast is poor.

## 6. Sensors & Interlocks
- Exhaust temperature (to confirm good combustion and detect problems)
- Water temperature (high-limit interlock)
- Optional: draft sensor or pressure switch
- CO alarm (separate, hard-wired or battery with alerting)

## 7. Status & Next Steps
Safety-critical principles and high-level dimensional guidance recorded. Full construction drawings and tested dimension tables are outside the scope of a single documentation pass and must be derived from established rocket-mass heater literature and local codes before any physical build.

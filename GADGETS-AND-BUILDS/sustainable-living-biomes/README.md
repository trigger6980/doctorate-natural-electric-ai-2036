# Sustainable Living Biomes

**Closed-loop living systems** that integrate the three off-grid hot-water generators with energy, water, food, and waste cycles.

## Core Loops
1. **Energy** — Solar thermal, biomass, PV, thermoelectric, and residual heat; managed by energy-aware agents.
2. **Water** — Rainwater capture, greywater treatment, hot-water generation, and return to productive use.
3. **Food** — Greenhouse, soil beds, or aquaponics that benefit from the thermal and nutrient outputs of the other loops.
4. **Waste / Resource** — Compost, optional biogas, and nutrient return to food production.

## Integration with Existing Portfolio Work
- Hot-water generators supply the thermal load.
- Energy-harvester-tinyml and Operator AI Machinery provide sensing, forecasting, and duty-cycle control.
- Off-grid AI box can host local knowledge and control interfaces without external connectivity.
- Security and integrity tools protect the control plane.

## Design Goals for a Minimal Viable Biome
- Support a small household or research station with hot water, basic electricity, and some food production.
- Remain fully functional during multi-day low-sun or low-biomass periods through storage and prioritization.
- Be documentable and reproducible by independent builders.
- Treat energy state, water inventory, and food production status as observable signals for intelligent agents.

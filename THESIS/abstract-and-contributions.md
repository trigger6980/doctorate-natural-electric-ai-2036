# Thesis Abstract & Claimed Contributions

**Working Title:** Natural Electric Foundations for Energy-Aware Agentic Intelligence at the Edge (2036 Horizon)

## Abstract (Draft)

The next decade of AI will be constrained less by model capability and more by energy, autonomy, and physical deployment. This work develops the theoretical and practical foundations of *Natural Electric* systems — ambient energy harvesting (magnetic, vibration, indoor/outdoor photovoltaic, RF, thermal) coupled with energy-state-aware TinyML and multi-agent orchestration — so that intelligent nodes can operate perpetually or near-perpetually without battery replacement or grid dependency. We present architectures, quantized models, reinforcement-learning control policies, and open hardware designs that treat harvested energy as a first-class runtime signal. The resulting systems enable off-grid AI boxes, self-powered sensor meshes, and sovereign edge compute suitable for remote, disaster, or intentionally disconnected environments. Contributions include open-source prototypes, evaluation methodologies under realistic energy traces, and a research roadmap toward neuromorphic and physical-AI realizations of the same principles.

## Claimed Contributions (to be validated experimentally)

1. Energy-state-aware inference scheduling that maintains >95% energy-neutral operation probability under indoor/outdoor stochastic irradiance (building on 2025–2026 hybrid PV+RF + TinyML results).
2. Portable multi-source (solar / hand-crank / battery / USB) AI-in-a-box reference design with local LLM capability and zero external connectivity after provisioning.
3. Desktop and edge security/networking components designed for high-speed, air-gapped, or intermittent-link environments.
4. Open agent orchestration patterns specialized for energy-constrained and partially-observable physical settings.
5. Reproducible hardware BOMs, schematics guidance, and software stacks that lower the barrier for independent researchers and home-lab builders.
